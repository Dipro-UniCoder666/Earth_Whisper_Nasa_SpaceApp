## Administrative reference

Bangladesh DPHE's *Jessore District Geo Code based Unique Water Point ID* table identifies the record `JESSORE 41 / MANIRAMPUR 61 / KULTIA 473 / SUJATPUR 368` on PDF page 72. This supports the administrative chain: Jessore (also written Jashore) District → Manirampur Upazila → Kultia Union → Sujatpur village. The code table is not a coordinate register, cadastral map, or fisheries enclosure inventory. [Official DPHE document](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-dphe/2024/12/8ddc502f31b5419089076326e526d691.pdf)

Its own disclaimer says its themes are for LGED internal use and are not authoritative for other uses; it is context only and was not digitized as authoritative geometry. [LGED map PDF](https://oldweb.lged.gov.bd/UploadedDocument/Map/KHULNA/jessore/monirampur/monirampur.pdf)

## Incident references

Dhaka Post (published 16 Jan 2026) reports the Sujatpur Pallimangal fish enclosure and a breach on its southern embankment, with reported flooding across Sujatpur, Hatgachha, Kultia, Moshiahati, Bedbhita and Balarabad. It supplies village context but no site coordinate, map, parcel, breach GPS, or polygon. [Dhaka Post, 16 Jan 2026](https://www.dhakapost.com/country/424238)

Samakal (published 16 Jan 2026) provides the most specific textual geography found: it places the named Pallimangal operation in Sujatpur mouza in Beel Kedaria, adjacent to Bhavadah; describes about 1,500 bigha leased by 19 joint lessees; and reports that a southern embankment section gave way while water entered from an adjacent khal. It reports a breach around 40–45 ft wide and 20 ft deep. These are reported descriptions, not measured GIS dimensions; the article gives no geodetic coordinates, plot ID, or named road/sluice landmark. The area figure cannot be used as an assumed enclosure polygon. [Samakal, 16 Jan 2026](https://samakal.com/whole-country/article/334020/%E0%A6%AA%E0%A6%BE%E0%A7%9C-%E0%A6%AD%E0%A7%99%E0%A6%99%E0%A7%87-%E0%A6%A2%E0%A7%81%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%B2-%E0%A6%AA%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BF-%E0%A7%A7%E0%A7%AB%E0%A7%AE-%E0%A6%B9%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%9F%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A6%9C%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BF)

Daily Country Today (published 17 Jan 2026) independently names the south-side embankment and says the event affected six villages including Sujatpur. [Daily Country Today, 17 Jan 2026](https://dailycountrytodaybd.com/bn/story/1%2C500-bighas-of-land-flooded-as-embankment-collapses%2C-over-500-families-homeless-in-jashore)

The official Kultia Union Parishad representative page lists a ward member with address in Sujatpur village, Moshiahati post office, Manirampur, Jashore, corroborating the administrative chain but not the fishery position. [Kultia Union Parishad](https://kultiaup.jessore.gov.bd/pages/leaders)

Confidence is moderate for the reported place-name context “Sujatpur mouza, Beel Kedaria, Bhavadah area” and low for an exact coordinate or parcel.

## Evidence needed to identify the enclosure

1. A GPS coordinate from the enclosure operator, local committee, union representative, or a field visit; alternatively a named road/canal/sluice landmark tied to the target.
2. Sujatpur mouza cadastral sheet, mouza/JL number, and parcel/plot reference, with CRS, sheet scale, survey vintage and digitization control points.
3. BWDB/IWM/CEIP/CEGIS or Department of Fisheries records tying the enclosure name to a map, asset/farm identifier, and boundary/embankment geometry.
4. Local reviewer-confirmed digitization against dated imagery, with reviewer identity, date, evidence links and confidence; independent field confirmation before any boundary is described as verified.
5. Separate field/reference polygons for the gher, neighboring aquaculture ponds, permanent water and surrounding cropland.

## Phase 5B cadastral search update (2026-11-12)

A targeted search of the Manirampur land-office mouza list found an entry for Sujatapur/Sujatpur at J.L. 143, with Hat Gachha 142 and Kultia 144 adjacent in that list. This strengthens the search lead and is consistent with the independently documented DPHE Sujatpur–Kultia–Manirampur chain. The list is a secondary source, however. **J.L. 143 is provisionally corroborated, not officially verified.** Use it as a search key only until the number is confirmed from the official cadastral sheet or DLRS index.

The Ministry of Land portal says its mouza map sheets are being digitized and georeferenced and exposes administrative, mouza, sheet and plot controls. Publicly accessible page content did not confirm Manirampur/Kultia/Sujatpur availability or return a Sujatpur map/plot. The Jessore Zonal Settlement Office site has an official village/mouza maps index and Manirampur selector, but the accessible page did not expose the Sujatpur sheet itself. ePorcha could not be inspected through this run's web access; its target-sheet availability remains unknown. These are retrieval limitations and are not evidence that the records do not exist.

No CS/SA/RS/BS/BRS sheet, original cadastral scan, plot schedule, or georeferenced GIS was recovered. Thus no survey version, scale, survey date, CRS, control points or residual error can be reported. Georeferencing was not attempted. Beel Kedaria, Pallimangal, the south-side khal and embankment are not spatially identified by the new evidence. No point or candidate enclosure polygon was created; the existing `not_delineated` status and null geometry remain unchanged.
